02 · Emergency Types

Know the threat. Know the move.

Tap a card to reveal the immediate action checklist.

🔥

Fire

Smoke, flames, burning smell

  • Pull nearest fire alarm
  • Evacuate via stairs — never elevators
  • Stay low under smoke
  • Call 101 & campus security
View full procedure →
🌍

Earthquake

Shaking, tremors, ground movement

  • Drop, Cover, Hold On
  • Stay away from windows
  • Evacuate after shaking stops
  • Assemble at safe zones
View full procedure →
❤️

Medical Emergency

Unconscious, bleeding, chest pain

  • Call 108 / campus medical
  • Check breathing & pulse
  • Control bleeding with pressure
  • Do not move injured person
View full procedure →
🌊

Flood / Waterlogging

Heavy rain, rising water

  • Move to higher ground
  • Avoid electrical equipment
  • Do not walk through flowing water
  • Switch off mains if safe
View full procedure →
⚠️

Threat / Violence

Active threat, suspicious activity

  • Run — evacuate if safe
  • Hide — lock & barricade
  • Fight — last resort
  • Call 100 / security silently

⚡

Power Outage

Sudden blackout, no electricity

  • Stay calm, use flashlight
  • Unplug sensitive devices
  • Use stairs, not elevators
  • Report to maintenance

03 · Step-by-Step

Detailed procedures.

Expand each section for the complete protocol.

  1. Alert: Pull nearest fire alarm and shout "FIRE".
  2. Assess: Small fire + trained? Use extinguisher (PASS). Else evacuate.
  3. Evacuate: Stairwells only. Never elevators. Stay low under smoke.
  4. Do not: Re-enter. No water on electrical/grease fires.
  5. Assembly: Proceed to assembly point. Report to warden.
  6. Call: 101 (Fire) and LPU Campus Security.
  1. During shaking: Drop, Cover, Hold On. Away from windows.
  2. Outdoors: Open area, away from buildings/power lines.
  3. Driving: Pull over, stop, parking brake. Avoid bridges.
  4. After: Evacuate calmly. Expect aftershocks. No elevators.
  5. Check: Injuries. Provide first aid.
  6. Do not: Candles/matches (gas). No elevators.
  1. Scene safety: Ensure safe area.
  2. Call: 108 / LPU Medical Centre. Give location & details.
  3. Responsiveness: Tap shoulder, ask "Are you okay?"
  4. Breathing: If not breathing, CPR (30:2).
  5. Bleeding: Firm direct pressure. Don't remove embedded objects.
  6. Do not: Food/water to unconscious. Don't move if spinal injury.
  1. Move up: Higher floors/ground. Avoid basements.
  2. Electricity: Switch off mains if safe.
  3. Avoid: Walking/driving through floodwater.
  4. Water safety: No tap water until cleared.
  5. Report: Hostel warden & maintenance.
  1. Run: Clear path? Evacuate. Leave belongings.
  2. Hide: Lock/barricade, silence phone, stay hidden.
  3. Fight: Last resort — improvise, commit.
  4. When safe: Call 100 / security. Give location.
  5. Do not: Pull fire alarm. Don't confront.
  1. Stay calm: Phone flashlight. No candles.
  2. Unplug: Sensitive electronics.
  3. Elevators: Don't use. If stuck, press alarm.
  4. Report: Maintenance / warden.
  5. Food: Keep fridge closed (~4 hrs safe).
04 · First Aid Quick Reference

Be the first responder.

Basic techniques everyone should know. Not a substitute for professional care.

🫁

CPR (Adult)

30 compressions (2 in deep) then 2 breaths. Rate 100-120/min.

🩸

Severe Bleeding

Firm direct pressure. Pack wound. Tourniquet last resort. Call 108.

🔥

Burns

Cool with running water 10-20 min. No ice/ointments. Sterile dressing.

🫁

Choking

5 back blows + 5 abdominal thrusts. Alternate until expelled/unconscious.

🐍

Snake / Insect Bite

Immobilize limb below heart. Don't cut/suck. Note snake. Rush to hospital.

🧠

Fainting

Lay flat, elevate legs. Loosen clothing. Check breathing. Call if >1 min.

05 · Safety Tips

Do's & Don'ts.

✅ Do

  • Know your nearest exit & assembly point
  • Save emergency numbers
  • Keep first-aid kit & flashlight
  • Report hazards immediately
  • Follow warden instructions
  • Keep exits clear

❌ Don't

  • Use elevators in emergencies
  • Spread rumors/panic
  • Block exits
  • Ignore fire drills
  • Handle hazards yourself
  • Re-enter until cleared
